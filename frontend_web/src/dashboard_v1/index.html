Flow Proxy Explanation

          
ETF flow, fund holdings, northbound flow, margin financing and block trades remain candidate or planned data types. Morningstar is treated as a licensed source candidate and no capital-flow data is fabricated.

          
    
            
  • ETF amount and volume are trading activity, not net inflow.
    • 
            
  • AUM change mixes market movement and flow.
    • 
            
  • Shares outstanding change is closer to creation/redemption but still needs source confirmation.
    • 
            
  • Holdings change shows allocation drift with disclosure lag.
