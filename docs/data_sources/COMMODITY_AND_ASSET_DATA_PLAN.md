# Commodity And Asset Data Plan

## Asset-Class Scope

Asset-class data includes:

- equity indices;
- government yields;
- FX and dollar index proxies;
- gold and precious metals;
- oil and energy;
- base metals;
- agriculture;
- funds and ETFs;
- commodities and futures.

## Commodity Rules

Commodity data must distinguish:

- spot;
- futures;
- benchmark;
- index;
- settlement;
- average price.

It must also distinguish venue/source:

- COMEX;
- NYMEX;
- LME;
- SHFE;
- INE;
- DCE;
- CZCE;
- World Bank;
- FRED;
- other/unknown.

World Bank Pink Sheet is a benchmark/average-price source. It must not be presented as exchange futures.

## Futures Framework

Raw single contracts and derived continuous contracts are different data products. Continuous futures must record construction method, roll rules, and adjustment method.

## Relation To Capital Flow

Asset-class data is often a price or risk-appetite proxy. It should not be stated as actual flow unless combined with flow-specific evidence.
