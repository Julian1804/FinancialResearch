# Current Research Mainline

## Mainline

The current FinancialResearch research mainline is:

> Research global capital flows and China-related capital flows.

This is the current priority, not the permanent only research topic.

## Core Questions

- Where does money come from?
- Which countries, asset classes, sectors, currencies, funds, ETFs, companies, and markets receive it?
- Which markets lose it?
- Which vehicles carry the flow: funds, ETFs, portfolios, holdings, derivatives, currencies, bonds, commodities, or direct equity exposure?
- How can price movement be separated from actual flow or proxy flow?
- How do changes in global liquidity, dollar conditions, rates, inflation, and policy affect China assets?
- How do those changes transmit into A-shares, H-shares, sectors, and companies?
- How does later data validate, falsify, or revise earlier forecasts?

## Not A Permanent Single Topic

FinancialResearch remains a general financial research platform. Capital-flow research is the current organizing thread because it connects macro, asset-class, fund/ETF, company, event, and forecast modules. Future work may introduce other research mainlines.

## Required Evidence Types

The current mainline needs:

- macro and policy context;
- rates, FX, USD liquidity, inflation, employment, and central-bank data;
- asset-class price and yield data;
- commodity prices and benchmark definitions;
- fund and ETF universe, NAV/returns, AUM, holdings, exposures, and flow proxies;
- A+H company market data, structured financials, announcements, and PDFs;
- news, policy, and event records;
- field-level trust and source provenance;
- forecast and backtest records.

## Interpretation Rules

- Flow proxy is not actual flow.
- ETF amount and volume are trading activity, not net inflow.
- AUM change mixes price return and flow.
- Holdings and exposure changes are delayed disclosure evidence.
- Estimated net flow can only be called flow when the source explicitly defines it.
- Candidate data must not be written as trusted fact.
