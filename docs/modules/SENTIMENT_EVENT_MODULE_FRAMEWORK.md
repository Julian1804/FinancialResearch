# Sentiment And Event Module Framework

## Purpose

The sentiment/event module captures policy events, company events, announcements, news, geopolitical events, and black-swan context.

## Source Classes

- Official announcements and filings.
- Exchange or regulator disclosures.
- Company news and notices.
- Institutional event sources.
- Media/news sources.
- Community or unverified sources.

## Trust Rules

Media and news sources are event, sentiment, or clue candidates. They are not institutional databases and do not directly provide trusted key facts without cross-source confirmation.

## Capital-Flow Relation

Events help explain why capital may move:

- policy shocks;
- rate or currency policy changes;
- sanctions and tariffs;
- war or geopolitical conflict;
- company scandals or strategy shifts;
- sector policy support or suppression.

## Report Usage

Events may be included in statistical reports and GPT packets with clear source and trust status. Missing source date or publish date should be flagged.
