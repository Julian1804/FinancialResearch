# Commodity Module Framework

## Purpose

The commodity module supports research into inflation, industrial demand, supply shocks, geopolitical risk, and asset-class rotation.

## Classification

Commodity categories:

- energy;
- precious metals;
- base metals;
- ferrous metals;
- agriculture;
- chemicals;
- livestock;
- softs;
- freight;
- other.

Price types:

- spot;
- futures;
- benchmark;
- index;
- settlement;
- average price.

Venues/sources:

- COMEX;
- NYMEX;
- LME;
- SHFE;
- INE;
- DCE;
- CZCE;
- World Bank;
- FRED;
- other/unknown.

## Futures Rule

Single-contract futures, front-month futures, settlement series, and continuous contracts are different. Continuous contracts are derived data and need roll/adjustment metadata.

## Capital-Flow Relation

Commodity prices may signal inflation, industrial demand, risk-off behavior, geopolitical stress, or sector pressure. They are not direct flow evidence alone.
