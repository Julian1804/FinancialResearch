# Asset Class Module Framework

## Purpose

The asset-class module tracks market prices and yields that proxy risk appetite, liquidity preference, inflation pressure, and capital allocation.

## Covered Areas

- FX and currencies.
- Dollar index and trade-weighted USD proxies.
- Sovereign yields.
- Rates and bond market indicators.
- Equity indices.
- Gold, oil, copper, and major commodities.
- Funds and ETFs.
- Futures and derivatives when schema-ready.

## Interpretation Rules

Asset-class price movement is not automatically capital flow. Price context must be combined with fund/ETF size, holdings, exposure, macro, and event data before making flow statements.

## Dashboard Requirements

The Dashboard should show:

- latest value;
- trend;
- quality and source status;
- publish/release date limitations;
- revision/vintage warnings;
- whether data is candidate, production candidate, or trusted-candidate preview.
