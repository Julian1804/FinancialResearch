# Global And China Capital Flow Mainline

## Purpose

This research mainline connects macro, asset-class, fund/ETF, commodity, company, event, forecast, and backtest modules to study global and China-related capital movement.

## Research Questions

- Where does money originate?
- Which currencies and funding markets matter?
- Which asset classes receive capital?
- Which countries and sectors receive or lose exposure?
- How are China assets affected by global dollar liquidity, US rates, Japan carry conditions, commodity shocks, and policy events?
- Which funds, ETFs, portfolios, holdings, or market instruments reveal the flow?
- Are observed signals actual flow, flow proxy, or only price movement?
- What later evidence confirms or revises a forecast?

## China-Specific Angle

China-related flow analysis must connect:

- RMB liquidity, M0/M1/M2, social financing, CPI/PPI, FX reserves, and policy signals;
- A-share market data and sector structure;
- H-share/HK exposure;
- funds, ETFs, public-fund disclosures, and Morningstar global exposure candidates;
- commodity and FX shocks;
- company fundamentals, announcements, and financial reports.

## Global Angle

Global flow analysis must connect:

- US rates, Treasury yields, Fed funds, CPI/PPI, employment, payrolls, and USD conditions;
- FX pairs and dollar index proxies;
- gold, oil, copper, and commodity benchmarks;
- global ETF and fund holdings, country exposure, sector exposure, and currency exposure;
- SEC filings and international company disclosures where available.

## Non-Goals

- Do not claim proxy data is actual flow.
- Do not equate price change with capital flow.
- Do not force a single data source to answer all questions.
- Do not make this mainline the only future research direction.
